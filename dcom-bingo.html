DCOM Bingo

Generate a new card, then tap a square whenever it appears in the movie. The center square is always free.

Why this exists

I watched every Disney Channel Original Movie with a friend, and we started compiling a list of the tropes we kept seeing along the way.

DCOM Bingo grew out of that list. It makes watching old, cliché-filled movies even more fun—and gives you something to look for when the plot starts feeling familiar.
