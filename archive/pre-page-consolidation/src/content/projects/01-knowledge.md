An organization’s knowledge is spread across documents, people’s experience, and the connections between teams. Finding what you need often means knowing who to ask.

Our agents are designed to capture those often hidden connections and help people navigate them.

I want people to reach the knowledge and colleagues they need while preserving the relationships through which that knowledge moves.
