Concept figure design study

Christopher selected the football playbook set (Option B) for the main homepage.
Its native SVG sources and scoped styles live in src/assets/figures/playbook/.
Standalone files with embedded local fonts export to public/figures/playbook/.
The diagrams contain no generated artwork or embedded raster images.

The selected set uses green coach's boards, circled people in formations, warm
chalk routes, blue AI interventions, coral risk marks, and amber coaching notes.
Preserve the original football routes, arrowheads, and line weights. Christopher
preferred them over a uniform geometry pass. Limit refinements to small changes
in one or two figures. Keep human connections direct, with AI beside the work.

Overview: AI helps people share knowledge, block cognitive risks, and coordinate
handoffs. People remain directly connected.
Knowledge: who knows what, when that knowledge is needed, and how people use it
to form collective judgement. Learning feeds back into the knowledge network.
Protection: cognitive risks stop at an AI block and shield, with a connected,
evenly spaced team formation behind it.
Coordination: direct routes between teams, a handoff, and a shared plan.

These are conceptual diagrams, not claimed architectures, guaranteed outcomes,
or measured results. Captions and SVG descriptions explain their purpose.
Source Sans 3 provides readable labels and Kalam supplies coaching notes. Fonts
are hosted locally and embedded in standalone exports, with their licenses.

Run pnpm figures to export both sets and regenerate preview.html (selected B)
and compare.html (A beside B). Serve this folder on localhost for design review.

The earlier Option A remains in src/assets/figures/, with a saved copy in
option-a/. Do not overwrite that snapshot while refining the selected set.
The untouched football baseline is saved in playbook-approved/.
